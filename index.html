Our History

Love God, love people, and make disciples of Jesus Christ.

Agape Ebenezer Karen Baptist Church is rooted in the rich spiritual heritage of the Karen people and their unwavering faith in Christ. Founded with a heart to serve the Karen community and beyond, our church stands as a beacon of hope, reconciliation, and transformation through the Gospel.

Our name carries deep meaning: "Agape" reflects God's unconditional love for all people, while "Ebenezer" (meaning "Stone of Help") reminds us of God's faithfulness through every season. We gather as one family, speaking both English and Karen, to worship Jesus Christ, grow in biblical knowledge, and serve our neighbors with compassion.

Through our ministries in worship, discipleship, and community outreach, we seek to embody Christ's love and extend His kingdom in Fort Worth and beyond. Our commitment is to be a place where all are welcomed, valued, and empowered to fulfill their God-given purpose.
